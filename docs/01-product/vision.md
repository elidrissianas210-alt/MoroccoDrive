# Product Vision

## Vision

Build the leading car rental marketplace and SaaS platform in Morocco, connecting customers with trusted rental agencies while providing agencies with modern digital tools to grow and manage their businesses.

Our long-term vision is to become the first destination for anyone looking to rent a car in Morocco and the preferred operating platform for professional rental agencies.

---

# Mission

Simplify the car rental experience for customers while helping agencies digitize their operations, increase their online visibility, attract more customers, and manage their business from one centralized platform.

---

# Problem Statement

## Agency Problems

Many car rental agencies in Morocco face several challenges:

- They do not have a professional website.
- They struggle to attract new customers.
- They rely heavily on WhatsApp, Facebook or phone calls.
- They lack a centralized platform to monitor revenue and business performance.

## Customer Problems

Customers also experience multiple frustrations:

- Comparing cars across different agencies is difficult.
- Searching through multiple websites and social media pages takes time.
- The booking process is often slow and inconvenient.

---

# Our Solution

Our platform combines two products into one ecosystem:

## Marketplace

Customers can:

- Search rental cars across Morocco.
- Compare offers from multiple agencies.
- Book vehicles through a modern experience.
- Find trusted rental agencies.

## SaaS Platform

Rental agencies can:

- Manage their fleet.
- Manage bookings.
- Track revenue.
- Generate contracts.
- Receive new customers through the marketplace.
- Operate their business from one dashboard.
