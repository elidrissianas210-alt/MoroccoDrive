# Agency Card

## Purpose

The Agency Card builds trust by presenting the rental agency behind the vehicle.

Its goal is to reassure customers before booking and highlight the professionalism of verified agencies.

---

## Used In

- Car Details Page
- Agency Profile
- Search Results
- Booking Summary

---

## Information Displayed

### Agency Identity

- Logo
- Agency Name
- Verified Badge

---

### Trust Indicators

- MoroccoDrive Trust Score
- Customer Rating
- Number of Reviews
- Years on MoroccoDrive
- Completed Rentals
- Average Response Time

---

### Services

- Airport Pickup
- Hotel Delivery
- Instant Booking
- Free Cancellation
- 24/7 Support

---

## Actions

Primary

- View Agency Profile

Secondary

- Contact Agency

---

## Business Rules

Verified agencies should always display the verification badge.

Trust Score must be visible.

Only public agency information should be displayed.

---

## Responsive Behavior

Desktop

Card

Mobile

Compact Card

---

## Accessibility

Keyboard navigation

Screen reader labels

Visible focus states

---

## Design Notes

The agency card should answer one question:

"Can I trust this agency?"
